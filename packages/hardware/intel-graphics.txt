# Intel integrated graphics. Selected when `lspci -k` shows a VGA/3D device
# whose kernel driver is in {i915, xe}.
#
# Why required: niri renders blur and the window animations through the Vulkan
# renderer. Without a matching Vulkan ICD, blur silently disappears and the
# compositor falls back to GL. libva-* additionally provides hardware video
# decode/encode for the Noctalia screen recorder.
vulkan-intel
libva-intel-driver
intel-media-driver
